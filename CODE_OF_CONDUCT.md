# PanganKita Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PanganKita contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community participants
should be able to collaborate professionally and respectfully regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or technical opinion.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and project-organized online collaboration. It does not impose additional
product functionality or change any technical licensing terms.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect, patience, and good faith.
- Offer specific, evidence-based technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and constructive disagreement; explain trade-offs and
  acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect accessibility needs, privacy, and individual boundaries.
- Credit the work of others; report copied or unlicensed material honestly.
- Help redirect misunderstandings into concrete, reviewable improvements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, bullying, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory remarks or attacks based on personal characteristics.
- Publication of another person's private information without consent.
- Targeted intimidation, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Fraudulent claims of authority, fabricated review evidence, or retaliation
  against someone for raising a good-faith concern.

## Reporting

For a **non-sensitive policy question**, use the repository's
[issue templates](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/) without naming private
individuals or disclosing incident details.

For abuse in a GitHub issue, pull request, or comment, use GitHub's
**Report content** menu. Where GitHub provides **Report to repository admins**,
you may select it; otherwise **Report abuse to GitHub Support**. This is a
platform reporting mechanism and is **not** a dedicated confidential
PanganKita conduct mailbox. See
[GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

**A private PanganKita conduct-reporting contact has not yet been designated.**
Do not put identifying information or sensitive evidence in public issues.
Maintainers should establish and publish a verified confidential contact and
conflict-of-interest path before representing internal incident reporting as
private or comprehensive.

For vulnerability disclosures, use the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md);
do not report exploit details as a conduct incident in public.

## Handling violations

Maintainers with applicable repository moderation permissions may, based on
available evidence and circumstances, request edits, issue warnings, hide or
remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. They should:

- assess reports fairly, without retaliation or assumptions of guilt;
- avoid sharing sensitive reports beyond people who need to respond;
- consider proportionality, context, recurrence, and user safety;
- avoid promising guaranteed anonymity, fixed response times, or actions
  beyond their actual permissions;
- use GitHub Support when GitHub-level safety intervention is required.

When a maintainer is involved in an incident, do not rely on that person as
the sole reviewer. GitHub's independent abuse-reporting mechanism remains
available; the project still needs a named internal conflict-of-interest
procedure.

## Reference

This project-specific policy is informed by the community principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) (version 3.0)
and GitHub's Community Guidelines. It is **not** a claim that PanganKita has
the Covenant's model reporting team or enforcement infrastructure already in
place.
